B. Amethyst Pop

The purple is a deep, saturated amethyst with an orchid-magenta partner. Every purple tested sits between 276 and 295 degrees on the colour wheel, so none of them can read as blue. Each one is shown big: the hero, the escrow band and the brands panel are all full blocks of purple. DM Serif Display gives the wordmark and headlines the thin-and-thick stroke look of Zara's logo, but it feels warmer and more fashionable, and Outfit keeps the small spaced-out labels clean, young and easy to read. The layout stays flat and magazine-like, and the copy stays short: shop now with no sign-up, your money is held until your order arrives, rep #LuxeBeauFam. C. Lavender Atelier

This version uses a calm, gallery-like layout close to Zara's, with lots of white space, thin 1px lines and square corners, so the clothes-shop elegance comes through. The colours are pinker than before so the page no longer looks blue: the main purple #8E24AA and the dark plum #5E1675 both sit at hue 285-288, and they fill the hero and the escrow safety band edge to edge. The fonts are new too: Italiana gives the LUXEBEAU name the thin-and-thick fashion-logo strokes, Cormorant Garamond gives big, warm magazine-style headlines, and Montserrat keeps the small menu and button text sharp and easy to read.
